💥1 Overview
Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs) are among the most widely used neural networks (NNs), and have been applied to many practical problems. MLPs require training tailored to specific applications, often encountering issues such as local minima, slow convergence, and sensitivity to initialization. This paper suggests using the recently developed Biogeography-Based Optimization (BBO) algorithm to train MLPs to mitigate these problems. To investigate the efficiency of BBO in training MLPs, five classification datasets and six function approximation datasets were used. The results were compared with five well-known heuristic algorithms: Backpropagation (BP) and Extreme Learning Machine (ELM) for capturing local minima, accuracy, and convergence rate. The findings indicate that training MLPs with BBO significantly outperforms current heuristic learning algorithms and BP. Furthermore, the results suggest that BBO can provide competitive results compared to ELM.
